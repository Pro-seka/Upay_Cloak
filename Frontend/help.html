How Upay_cloak works, from a payment to a closed case.

How the system works

1
Score every transaction

Each transfer gets a risk score from 0 to 1. The score comes from signals such as a new device, odd hours, an amount far from the customer's usual, and links to risky wallets. Every signal shows how much it raised or lowered the score.

2
Decide what happens to the money

Under 0.30 the transfer is allowed. From 0.30 to 0.64 the customer gets a warning, and a risky cash-out is held. From 0.65 to 0.79 the customer must confirm with a PIN. At 0.80 and above the transfer is blocked.

3
Open a case for risky activity

High and critical transactions, and known scam scenarios, become cases. Each case answers three questions: what happened, why it is risky, and what to do next.

4
Investigate and act

Open a case to see the timeline, the reasons behind the score and the connected wallets. Ask the assistant a question, then choose Hold, Block, Request KYC, Escalate or Mark as false positive. Every action is saved in the audit trail.

5
Warn the customer

The Warning demo shows what a customer sees in the upay app before sending money, next to the scoring behind it.

Alert types

Account takeover
Someone else is using a customer's account, often from a new device.
Mule network
Many accounts pass stolen money to one wallet, then cash out fast.
Scam
A customer is tricked into sending money.
Agent anomaly
An agent's activity is far from what similar agents do.
Step-up
An extra check, such as a PIN, before the money moves.
